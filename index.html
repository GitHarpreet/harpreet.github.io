About Me

Welcome! I am a third-year PhD student in the Department of Economics at McGill University.

I am interested in development, financial frictions, and structural models. My research explores how credit access, political institutions, and social identity shape household and firm decisions. I am passionate about combining data, theory, and field insights to study inequality, and labor and firm productivity.

I received the doctoral training award from the Quebec Government (FRQSC) in 2024. Before joining the PhD program I worked as a senior Research Associate at the PGRP team in J-PAL South Asia. I also worked as a Research Fellow at the Verghese Kurien Policy Lab at Institute of Rural Management Anand. I obtained my Masters from Delhi School of Economics and my Bachelor's degree in Physics and Mathematics.

I write bi-weekly on Substack about Economics in day-to-day life, and about PhD admissions and PhD life — read it here. Feel free to book time with me to talk about research, sports, or anything interesting.
